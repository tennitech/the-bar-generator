How this network works

Input mix t sets [t, 1 − t, 2t − 1]. Each unit applies the selected activation to its weighted input sum, with zero bias. Tanh center areas map −1 to +1; sigmoid areas map 0 to 1. Input areas always map −1 to +1.

Weight sets are repeatable random initializations. This untrained model has no dataset, learning or prediction task. Local neighbors masks the dense matrix to nearby units.

Secondary Color Usage

  • Bold brand colors (RPI red, blue, and gold) should generally be used as background color fills. Don't mix these bold brand colors together in a single composition (poster, Instagram story, etc.). For a series, you can use different bold colors across individual compositions.
  • Use the neutral colors (gray, silver) to ground compositions and allow bolder brand colors to be used for emphasis.
  • Typography should generally be black and white for legibility, but bolder colors can be applied to small type and to create typographic texture in the background.
  • Don't allow the secondary palette to outshine the primary brand colors. Across RPI communications, use red as the dominant brand color, with blue and gold as supporting colors. While red should be the most frequently used and prominently featured brand color in the totality of RPI's visual presence, individual compositions can certainly use blue or gold without red.
