# ASCII entrance review states

Captured November 4, 2026 from the local application in Chromium at 1280 × 800.
`home.png` and `generator.png` show one intermediate sampled ASCII frame, not the
final resting interface. The transition resolves to the unchanged artwork/text.

These captures contain the repository's existing sample artwork, fonts and logo
geometry. No external artwork or user-supplied private content was added. The
underlying asset-permission questions in `references/asset_licensing.md` remain
open; these captures are implementation evidence, not formal RPI approval.

`node scripts/asciiEntranceSmoke.js` reproduces desktop/mobile captures and checks
cleanup, reduced motion, interruption and preparation timing. Its browser and
CPU-throttle options are listed in README.md. Headless measurements are not a
physical-device, battery, or assistive-technology certification.
