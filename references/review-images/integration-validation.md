# Integration review images

Captured October 27, 2026 from the combined application with the Share menu fully open:

- `integration-desktop.png`: Chromium, 1440 × 900, Black ink theme.
- `integration-mobile.png`: Chromium touch emulation, 390 × 844, White ink theme.

These show the existing repository-provided logo/font assets and the pinned Feather
UI glyphs. No replacement identity asset, stock image or new font is introduced.
Underlying asset permission questions in [asset licensing](../asset_licensing.md)
remain unchanged. Screenshots and automated measurements do not establish owner
acceptance, physical-device testing or formal RPI approval.

Across 24 theme/viewport captures, toolbar neighbors had identical top alignment;
inter-control gaps were the established 8px mobile / 10px desktop values. Open
Share menus, readable labels and the relationship between the mark and controls
were reviewed in full-page context. The screenshots are representative states,
not proof of every interaction or continuous motion quality.
