# RPI Brand Hub source archive

Captured through the user's signed-in browser on November 17, 2026. The
[operational policy](../brand_policy.md) routes changes to the relevant full
pages. Root AGENTS.md remains the canonical project instruction file.

## Coverage and scope

The agreed scope contains **17 authored guideline pages**, **seven library landing
pages**, and **12 inspected collection pages**. All 25 top-level navigation
entries are accounted for: 24 were captured and The Bar Generator was explicitly
excluded by the owner. The 12 collection captures are additional visits. The
remaining logo catalog was excluded after the owner asked to skip logos.

No individual asset records were opened as part of the agreed scope, and no
logo, font, image, audio or template files were downloaded. One logo collection
had already been inspected before the logo-catalog exclusion. External request
forms/sites, media transcripts and image-only text are excluded; no form was
submitted. Section anchors within a captured guideline are covered by that
page's complete scroll capture, rather than counted as separate pages. Linked
short URLs naming the same destinations are not counted as new content.

The [manifest](2026-11-17/manifest.json) records every captured URL, date, type,
text length, source file hash and exclusion. The
[navigation inventory](2026-11-17/navigation.json) retains the discovered root
navigation. Nothing here claims access to unpublished, unlinked or unauthorized
pages, or that the underlying asset inventory was copied.

## How to use the files

Each `.md` is a reading copy of authored page text with exact repeated lines
removed across scroll positions. Each `.json` retains provenance, headings,
alternative text, sanitized links and ordered raw scroll-frame text. Repeated
values in tables/font specimens can disappear from the deduplicated reading
copy; use the raw frames and official source when their relationships matter.
Read the actual image/diagram or media if a decision depends on visual content:
alt text is evidence of a description, not a replacement for the graphic.

The page content loads progressively. Every guideline was moved through its
scrollable area to the bottom and captured at successive positions so offscreen
examples were included. JSON records preserve raw DOM text as well as rendered
text; transient UI/placeholder labels are not new official design requirements.
Library/collection records omit individual asset catalog listings and distinguish
catalog headings from normative brand guidance. Some collections expose no
additional prose; that is recorded without inventing usage or license terms.

## Refresh and redistribution

Navigate the live hub with an authorized account, inventory navigation and
collections again, scroll each authored guideline through its full content,
and preserve the exact source URL, displayed modification date, capture time,
text and image descriptions. Do not use stored session cookies/tokens in scripts.
Use a new dated folder for a new capture; retain earlier evidence. Account for
new pages and exclusions explicitly, refresh manifest hashes, point
`brand_rules.json` at the intended active snapshot, review the conflict register,
and rerun the brand guard and tests. A new page is not covered until its actual
text has been reviewed and routed into policy.

RPI source text retains its copyright and is not relicensed by the project's
MIT software license. User-authorized access/capture does not establish general
public redistribution rights for text, trademarks, fonts or artwork. Keep the
[permission record](../asset_licensing.md) open and confirm the publication scope
before distributing this captured corpus. On November 17 the project owner
explicitly requested committing this complete package, opening a PR and merging
it if checks pass. That records publication intent for this repository; it does
not create a general asset license or formal RPI approval of generator output.
