## Page text

A brand built for discovery.

Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute is the original technological university. We’ve been inventing the future for over 200 years. Our brand is a framework for discovery — where one idea can change everything.

Our logo reflects how the world knows us: as RPI. It’s anchored by a dynamic bar that can encode patterns generated from data, math, and art.

The bar is a simple but powerful design component. It’s a dynamic stage for celebrating what we do with the foundational elements of our respective fields. Doubling patterns that represent exponential growth, Venn diagrams, units of measure, binary code, and even musical scores — in the hands of our community, the combinations are infinite.

The bar reflects the diversity and curiosity of the people and organizations that make up RPI, while inviting the question that drives us forward together: “What if we tried this?”

We have brand colors for every context. They’re rooted in history, optimized for impact, and designed for accessibility.

Our typography system is designed for technical precision, flexibility, and distinction.

This Brand Hub will be updated with new information as it becomes available.
