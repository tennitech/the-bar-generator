## Page text

The Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute seal serves as our most formal mark of authentication, reserved for occasions requiring the highest level of institutional gravitas and authority.

University seals exist to authenticate — to formally validate documents and ceremonies with institutional authority. RPI’s seal, rooted in our 200-year heritage, carries the weight of tradition and the responsibility of official representation.

The seal is not a logo. It is a mark of authentication that validates the most important moments in our institutional life: graduation, formal recognition, and official documentation. Its power lies in restraint. By reserving it for moments of true institutional significance, we preserve its authority and meaning.

APPROVED USAGE

Primary Applications

Diplomas and academic certificates

Commencement programs and ceremonial materials

Official university contracts and legal documents

Awards and recognition certificates of institutional significance

Ceremonial Applications

Presidential podium and formal speaking occasions

Board of Trustees materials and official governance documents

Institutional dedications and cornerstone ceremonies

Official university regalia and academic dress

All seal usage requires prior approval through the Marketing and Communications Request Form. The seal should never appear alongside commercial applications, promotional materials, or casual communications.

To request seal usage:

Include context, audience, and intended use.

Allow minimum of 10 business days for review.

Obtain written approval before production.

TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS

Minimum Size Requirements

Print: 1.5” diameter minimum

Digital: 150px diameter minimum

Embossed: 2” diameter minimum

Color Variations

Single color: Black, white, or institutional red only

Embossed: Gold foil or blind emboss for premium applications

DON’TS

The seal and primary RPI logo share a meaningful semiotic connection while serving distinct institutional purposes. The surveyor’s rod — central to the 1904 seal design — inspired the measurement bar system in the contemporary RPI logo. This visual DNA creates continuity between formal authentication and daily brand expression. The radial tick marks in the updated seal echo the linear measurement system of the primary logo, creating a cohesive visual language that spans from ceremonial gravitas to modern communication.

Seal: Authentication, ceremony, official documentation

Logo: Communication, brand recognition, daily operations

This shared visual heritage ensures institutional continuity while allowing each mark to fulfill its specific role. When both appear in the same application (such as Commencement materials), their relationship reinforces RPI’s commitment to precision, measurement, and technical excellence across all contexts.

Don’t use the seal for marketing or promotional materials.

Don’t combine the seal with the primary logo lockup.

Don’t use the seal below minimum size requirements.

Don’t apply the seal to merchandise or casual communications.

Don’t modify the seal design or proportions.

Don't modify the colors for the seal.

Don’t use the seal without obtaining approval from the President’s Office.
