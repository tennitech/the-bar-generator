## Page text

These marks are used at certain events to embody RPI’s competitive spirit and school pride. They are designed to promote Institute athletics and generate community energy — not to replace our primary logo or represent academic programs.

ATHLETIC MARKS

RPI’s athletic wordmarks provide bold, energetic branding for sports teams and athletic events. Available in both “Rensselaer” and “Engineers” versions, these marks create immediate recognition and school pride for athletes and fans.

Usage

Team uniforms and athletic apparel

Sports marketing and promotional materials

Arena signage and venue branding

Fan merchandise and spirit wear

Approval Process

Use of Athletic marks must be approved by Adrian Donnelly, Assistant Athletics Director, donnea3@rpi.edu.

PUCKMAN

Puckman is RPI’s beloved hockey mascot — an anthropomorphic hockey puck wearing an engineer’s helmet. This character embodies the technical precision and competitive spirit that defines RPI athletics, particularly our championship hockey tradition.

Role

Puckman serves as a rallying symbol for RPI athletics, bringing personality and energy to our sports programs. This mascot complements — but never replaces — our primary logo system.

With prior approval, Puckman is to be used only for youth apparel, special events, and hockey-related items. This logo is a stand-alone mark and should not be used in conjunction with text and other logos.

Hockey-specific branding and merchandise

Athletic event promotion and fan engagement

Spirit wear and commemorative items

Social media and digital athletics content

Use of Puckman requires approval through the Athletics Department brand review process.
