## Page text

Follow these steps to create your RPI email signature in Outlook.

In Outlook, go to the File tab and click Options.

Click on Mail in the Outlook Options listing at the left.

Under Compose Messages, click on the Signatures button.

In the Email Signature tab, click New.

Type in a name for the new signature, and click OK.

Copy and paste the following template into the Edit Signature field, personalizing the appropriate information:

[Name]

[Title]

[Department]

RPI

[Email]

[Phone]

[110 8th St, Building, Floor 00]

Troy, NY 12180

rpi.edu

Select all the text and choose the RPI Geist font and bold your name and RPI. Note: You will have to download and install the RPI Geist font. If RPI Geist is not available, please use Source Sans Pro.

Optional: Add the RPI logo (downloadable below) to the bottom of your new signature: Click on the Insert Image icon, select the RPI logo .png file, and click Insert.

Click Save.

Under Choose default signature, use the drop-down menu to select your newly-created signature.

When you’ve finished, click OK, and then OK again to exit the Outlook Options window.
