## Page text

This section captures the essence of who we are as an institution — our driving purpose, our community, and the values that guide everything we do.

OUR PURPOSE AND VISION

Who We Are

Founded in 1824, Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute (RPI) is the first technological research university in the United States. We bring creativity, science, and technology together to address society’s greatest challenges.

Our Purpose

We cultivate exceptional problem-solvers by immersing them in a culture of rigorous inquiry, disciplined practice, and hands-on application. Our strategic plan, RPI Forward, charts a pioneering course for the next era of RPI.

Our Vision and Roadmap for the Future

By 2034, RPI will redefine education and research in the fields of science and technology.

Explore the RPI Forward plan. ↗︎

OUR CORE VALUES

When the experiment fails at 2 a.m. and your lab partner shows up with coffee, you discover what fortitude really means. We don’t just bounce back — we adapt, persist, and find new paths through problems that would stop others cold. Our distinctiveness isn't polished or predictable. We're the ones who see solutions where others see impossibilities. We embrace being different.

Creativity lives in our hands as much as our minds. We don’t just theorize — we build, test, break, and rebuild until the idea becomes reality, often using whatever materials we can find. This restless curiosity drives us to ask better questions, to collaborate across disciplines, and to stay confident in our abilities without losing sight of how much we still don't know.

The impact we create ripples outward. Discoveries that started here change how the world works, and innovations that began as student projects transform entire industries. Excellence for us isn't perfection; it's the obsessive attention to detail that turns good ideas into great ones, the focus that sees projects through to completion.

We are a community of distinctively-wired minds who understand that the best solutions emerge when diverse thinkers work together. Through partnership — with each other, with our neighbors, with organizations near and far — we multiply our capabilities and extend our reach, knowing that the challenges worth solving require more than any one person or organization can offer.

OUR COMMUNITY

Students are the heart of our culture.

They don’t just consume knowledge — they actively shape it. We address them as co-creators in their education, respecting their unique approaches to learning while challenging them to exceed their own expectations.

Faculty and staff are the architects of our culture.

Faculty serve as both mentors and fellow explorers, bringing deep expertise while remaining curious about new possibilities. Staff provide the essential foundation that enables discovery to flourish. Together, they embody our principle of “challenging intentionally” by pushing students beyond their comfort zones while providing the support necessary for breakthrough discoveries.

Prospective students represent the future of our community.

We seek those who are intellectually driven and want more than a credential — they crave transformation, impact, and meaning. We address them honestly about both the challenges and extraordinary opportunities that await.

Alumni are living proof of RPI’s enduring impact.

They carry our experimental ethos into their careers and communities, continuing to solve problems with the resilience and creativity fostered here. We celebrate them not just as successful graduates, but as ongoing contributors to RPI's legacy of innovation and as bridges connecting our achievements to possibilities.

External collaborators extend our influence beyond campus borders.

From industry partners to community organizations, these collaborators bring real-world context to our work and help ensure that our innovations address genuine societal needs. We engage them as essential partners in our mission to apply science to the common purposes of life.
